Annual Community Health Outreach Summary

The community health outreach program completed its third year of operation
in March 2026, reaching an estimated 42,000 residents across the Northern
Province. Vaccination uptake improved significantly, rising from 61% at the
start of the program to 88% by the end of the reporting period.

Program Highlights

Mobile clinics visited 54 villages during the year, a 20% increase over the
previous year's 45 villages. The average clinic visit served approximately
78 patients, with wait times reduced from 45 minutes to 22 minutes following
the introduction of an appointment scheduling pilot in October 2025.

Funding and Partnerships

The program was funded jointly by the Ministry of Health and three
international partner organizations, with a combined budget of
approximately $2.4 million for the year. Partner contributions accounted
for 65% of total funding, with the remainder covered by government grants.

Outlook

Planning for next year focuses on expanding mobile clinic coverage to the
Western Province and increasing the vaccination target to 95% coverage by
December 2026. Staff training on the new scheduling system will begin in
January 2026.
